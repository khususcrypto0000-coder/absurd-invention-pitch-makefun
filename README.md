## The pitch

The entry is a single sentence (175 characters) pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance: the microwave that keeps beeping at food you forgot about. Instead of nagging you forever, this microwave takes matters into its own hands — it quietly eats one spoonful of your lunch for every minute you ignore it, so the meal is always served hot and on time, just mysteriously a little smaller.

The joke lives in the invention itself: a kitchen appliance that solves
your forgetfulness by taxing your dinner. It is silly, yet weirdly
plausible — a machine that punishes procrastination one spoonful at a time.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the one-sentence entry to
`entry.txt` inside the output directory:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It honours the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable when set, and
otherwise writes to `/output/entry.txt`. The program exits 0 on success.
